2026-08-19AmberFalls, LT

Storage availability reaches 250 MWh

The latest pumped-storage upgrade at AmberFalls brings dispatchable capacity to 250 MWh — the figure set out for 2026 in our sustainability plan. The extra headroom is used for peak support and frequency response on the regional grid.

2026-07-30Baltics

Night rate of €0.09/kWh opens to all metered households

Any customer with a smart meter can now move to the time-of-use tariff and pay €0.09/kWh between 22:00 and 07:00 on weekdays, and €0.10/kWh at weekends.
