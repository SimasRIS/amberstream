About us

We generate power, and we keep the grid that carries it standing up.

AmberStream Energy operates and develops transmission networks across the Baltic region. Our focus is dependable delivery, grid safety, and practical innovation that benefits homes, businesses and industry.

{# ---------- The dam ---------- #}

AmberFalls

A hydro storage plant that works as a battery.

Our hydro storage assets help stabilise the grid: water is pumped uphill and stored when demand is low, then released through turbines when demand peaks. That makes AmberFalls both a generator and one of the largest storage devices in the region.

The plant's 174 MW rating describes instantaneous power — the output at a given moment, not energy per hour. Running at full output it produces roughly 4,176 MWh in a 24-hour period.

Key numbers

AmberFalls in figures

Installed capacity 174 MW (174,000 kW)
Instantaneous output Power at a moment in time — not energy “per hour”
Energy at full output 4,176 MWh / day
Estimated annual generation 762,120 MWh / yr (~50% capacity factor)
Homes powered ~190,530–254,040 / yr (3–4 MWh per home)
CO₂ avoided ~228,636 tonnes / yr (vs. 300 kg/MWh grid)
