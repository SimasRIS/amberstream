{# ---------- The dam ---------- #}

AmberFalls hydroelectric dam

One dam. Roughly a quarter of a million homes.

AmberFalls stores water behind the dam and releases it through turbines on demand. Its 174 MW rating is instantaneous power — the output at a moment in time, not energy per hour.

174 MW Installed capacity
762,120 MWh Generated per year at ~50% capacity factor
228,636 t CO₂ avoided per year vs. a 300 kg/MWh grid
190k–254k Homes powered annually (3–4 MWh each)

{# ---------- Why us ---------- #}

Why AmberStream

A small Baltic utility that owns its own generation.

Local and renewable

Every kilowatt-hour we sell is generated at AmberFalls, not bought on a spot market and relabelled.

Transparent pricing

One published rate per plan. Network charges and taxes stay on their own line of your bill.

Grid stability

SCADA-controlled hydro storage supplies peak support and frequency response to the regional grid.

Support that answers

A small team in Vilnius, reachable Mon–Fri 09:00–17:00, plus a 24/7 outage hotline.
